From “Time to BeReal” to “Let Me Post My BeFake:” A Case of Operationalizing Authenticity Through Design

Elizabeth Li, Annika Pinch, Justine Fisher, Darren Gergle, Jeremy Birnholtz · CSCW 2025 (PACM HCI 9, 7)

Best Paper Honorable Mention

Through interviews with 25 BeReal users, we find that participants experienced facets of authenticity that often didn’t align with BeReal’s rigid operationalization of it, and that their desire for authenticity was in tension with privacy, autonomy, and the wish to reflect back on significant moments.
